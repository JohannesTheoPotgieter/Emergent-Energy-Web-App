- Look across sheets for anchor phrases and header candidates.
- Once detected, identify header row by the row with the highest density of known header words.
- Determine table bounds by scanning contiguous non-empty rows after header until termination condition.

COLUMN NORMALIZATION (CANONICAL FIELDS)

- known synonyms + prior MappingRules for this TemplateProfile

CONFIDENCE + REVIEW LOGIC
