You are the senior product engineer + QA lead for Emergent Energy’s Program Dashboard web app.

Goal
Fix and fully replicate the Tracker sheet “Expenditure Breakdown” so it works in the app exactly like Excel.
UI can improve, but do NOT change the meaning of any numbers, date logic, or totals.
If parsing confidence is low, show RAW SHEET VIEW rather than empty tables.

Business meaning (non-negotiable)
This screen is the operational source of truth for project COS and cashflow.
- PO capture = procurement commitment + reference
- Invoice capture = supplier invoicing event
- Invoice Raised Date = COS realisation date (the date COS is recognised)
- Finance Payment Date = cash out date (cashflow)
These dates must feed the rest of the app:
- Finance - COS must use Invoice Raised Date for COS timing
- Cashflow must use Finance Payment Date for expenditure cash timing
- Actual Expenditure totals must reconcile to the tracker (Bolt Ave sum equals 2,217,962.171875)

Sheet structure reality (Bolt Ave reference)
The Expenditure Breakdown sheet contains two side-by-side tables:
A) COSTED/BUDGET side (left) headers include:
- No., Product/Service, Description of Work, QTY, Rate/Unit, Budget Total, FORECASTED PAYMENT DATE, Total COS
B) ACTUAL/FINANCE side (right) headers include:
- No., Product/Service, Description of Work, QTY, Rate/Unit, Actual Total, PO NUMBER, INVOICE NUMBER,
  INVOICE RAISED DATE, FINANCE PAYMENT DATE, Total COS
There are also category subtotal rows and blank separator rows.

Non-negotiable outcomes
1) The app must show ALL imported costed and actual line items from the sheet (including categories and subtotals), with correct totals.
2) The following fields must be editable and persisted in DB without breaking imported baseline:
   - PO Number
   - Invoice Number
   - Invoice Raised Date (COS realisation date)
   - Finance Payment Date (cashflow date)
   - Optional: comments/notes per line
3) The app must compute and display correct summaries that match Excel meaning:
   - Total Budget (sum of Budget Total)
   - Total Actual (sum of Actual Total)  <-- must reconcile to Excel
   - Variance (Actual - Budget) and/or (Budget - Actual) (choose one and label clearly)
   - COS Realised (sum of Actual Total where Invoice Raised Date is present)
   - Cash Paid (sum of Actual Total where Finance Payment Date is present)
   - Outstanding Invoices / Unpaid (invoiced but not paid)
   - Committed (PO exists but not yet invoiced)  (if useful, optional)
4) Filtering and usability must be world-class:
   - Filters: Category, Supplier/Invoice text search, Missing PO, Missing Invoice, Invoiced not paid, Paid, Date range (FY)

   - Quick action: edit PO/invoice/date inline (fast) or via side panel
5) Data integrity rules:
   - Imported sheet data is baseline and must remain recoverable.
   - Manual edits must not corrupt baseline.
   - Replace upload must not delete other projects.

     - Preserve manual edits when line identity matches, otherwise reset with warning.
   - No silent failure. Show parse diagnostics and mapping.

Excel parsing requirements (do not break again)

  - Handle merged/title rows and whitespace.
  - Handle duplicate headers (index them).
- Expenditure Breakdown has two tables side-by-side. Parser must detect both and merge into one canonical dataset.
- Extract row types:
  - category header (e.g., “1. Panels”)
  - line item (real item rows)
  - subtotal/total row
  - blank row
