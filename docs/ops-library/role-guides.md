# Role Guides (Active)

## Purpose
Define operational authority and expected actions by role in live workflows.

## Roles
### Project Development
- Owns pre-handover opportunity execution readiness.
- Ensures CRM-aligned commercial context is complete.
- Must not mark PM-owned work complete post-handover.

### Project Management
- Owns delivery execution after PD handover.
- Maintains evidence quality, schedule integrity, and stage transitions.
- Coordinates Compliance/HSE engagement once handover is accepted.

### Compliance / HSE
- Owns post-handover compliance workflows and safety evidence integrity.
- Blocks unsafe advancement where requirements are incomplete.

### Finance / Commercial Operations
- Owns PO/invoice/payment integrity and report trust escalation.
- Enforces COS and revenue realization rules.

### Admin / Platform Operator
- Owns permissions, feature toggles, recovery paths, and audit visibility.
- Cannot use admin privilege to bypass data trust controls without explicit logged reason.

## Access principles
- Least privilege by default.
- Explicit separation of operational and administrative powers.
- All elevated actions require durable audit logging.
