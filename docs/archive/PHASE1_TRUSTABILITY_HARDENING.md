# Phase 1 Trustability and Safety Hardening Implementation Plan

## Introduction  
This document outlines the implementation plan for Phase 1 of the Trustability and Safety Hardening project aimed at enhancing the security and reliability of our systems.

## Objectives  
1. To identify vulnerabilities and risks within the current system architecture.  
2. To establish a framework for ongoing trustability assessments.  
3. To implement safety hardening measures effectively.

## Scope  
The scope of Phase 1 includes:  
- Security assessments  
- Policy and procedure adjustments  
- Development of trustability metrics

## Implementation Steps  
1. **Risk Assessment**  
   - Conduct a thorough review of existing systems.  
   - Identify and document potential vulnerabilities.  

2. **Framework Development**  
   - Develop a Trustability Assessment Framework.  
   - Ensure that the framework aligns with industry best practices.
   
3. **Policy Review and Adjustment**  
   - Review current safety policies.  
   - Update and enhance policies as per identified risks.

4. **Training and Awareness Programs**  
   - Conduct training sessions for staff on trustability and safety practices.  
   - Create awareness materials to promote security culture.

5. **Implementation of Hardening Measures**  
   - Apply technical controls to harden systems against identified vulnerabilities.  
   - Establish monitoring mechanisms.

6. **Monitoring and Reporting**  
   - Set up systems for ongoing monitoring of trustability measures.  
   - Regular reporting of compliance and risk levels.

## Timeline  
- **Start Date**: 2026-03-15  
- **End Date**: 2026-06-15

## Conclusion  
This implementation plan serves as a roadmap to enhance the trustability and safety of our systems in Phase 1. Regular updates and evaluations will ensure that we meet our security objectives effectively.
