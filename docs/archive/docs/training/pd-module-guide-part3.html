8. The Handover Workflow

The handover is how a project moves from PD into PM execution. It's a structured checklist ensuring nothing falls through the cracks.

8.2 What PD Needs to Fill In

The handover page is divided into sections. Here's what each section requires:

Handover Fields

Deliverables (3 Required Documents)

🛑
All 15+ blockers must be cleared before you can submit The system checks every field and deliverable. Missing items are shown in a red blocker list at the top of the page. You must fix ALL of them before the Submit button works.

1

Fill in all handover fields

Complete PD Owner, PM Owner, Scope Summary, Risks, Assumptions, Engineering Status, and all readiness fields.

2

Upload all 3 deliverables

Add reference links and dates for Handover Charter, Site Visit Report, and Signed Cost Proposal. All 3 must be marked complete.

3

Save Draft regularly

Click "Save Draft" to save your progress. You can come back and continue later.

4

Clear all blockers

Check the red blocker list at the top. Fix each missing item until the list is empty.

5

Click "Submit for PM Review"

Once all blockers are cleared, this button activates. Click it to send the handover to PM for review. PM reviewers are automatically notified.

8.4 Step-by-Step: PM Reviewing a Handover

ℹ️
Who can review? Project Manager Program Manager COO / CEO / Admin
1

Open the handover

You'll receive a notification when a handover is submitted. Click the notification or navigate to the handover page.

2

Review everything

Check all handover fields, deliverables, evidence score, dependencies, and risks.

3

Accept or Reject

Accept: Click "Accept Handover". The project moves to "PM Active" phase and execution is enabled.
Reject: Click "Reject Handover", provide a clear reason, and click "Submit Rejection". PD is notified and can fix issues and resubmit.

Click the "Copy Metrics" button (top-right). This copies a formatted text summary of all metrics to your clipboard. Paste it into your board pack document, email, or presentation.

10.1 What Triggers a Notification?

EventWho Gets Notified
Handover submitted for reviewAll PM reviewers
Handover acceptedPD owner
Handover rejectedPD owner (includes rejection reason)
Engineering task assignedAssigned engineer

10.2 How to Use Notifications

  • Red badge on bell = you have unread notifications (number shown)
  • Click the bell to open the notification panel
  • Click a notification to navigate to the relevant page (ticket, handover, or task)
  • "Mark all read" clears the unread count
  • Notifications auto-refresh every 30 seconds
